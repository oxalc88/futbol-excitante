- **builder_agent:** builder-structured

- **hypothesis:** The last COMMON-BOUNDS residual (a defending body at |x| = 52.5308 m against the declared 52.5 m pitch half-length, on the accepted gk-shot-fixture run under the core-owned lifecycle) is a **goal-depth-geometry** case, not a genuinely illegal position: the offending body is the team-b designated keeper, pushed into its own goal mouth by a player-player contact with the attacking chaser after a goal, and it is inside its own nominal goal arc. Therefore the declared bound needs the goal-mouth geometry derived from the versioned `gk-small-sided-v1` goal-arc constant (`goal_arc_radius`), widened as a geometry correction — never an invented PES constant, and never an oracle weakening.

## Root-cause conclusion

**Path (b): the declared bound needs goal-depth geometry.**

- **Who:** `player-10` (team-b), the designated small-sided keeper (`keeperByTeam = {"team-a":"player-4","team-b":"player-10"}`).
- **Where:** tick 399 (argmax; the exact value 52.530848… plateaus over ticks 399–450), `x = 52.531 m`, `y = 0.695 m`, speed 0, `distToBall = 0.563 m`. The keeper is between the posts (`|y| < 3.66`) and behind the goal line (`x > 52.5`), inside its own nominal goal arc (distance to the arc centre = 0.696 m < `goal_arc_radius` 4.0 m).
- **Why:** at tick 391 a `goal` event fires (ball crosses the team-b goal line at `x = 52.5`). The attacking chaser (`player-1`) follows the loose ball into the goal mouth and the core `player-player-contact` resolution separates the two bodies over ticks 391–399, pushing the keeper back to `x = 52.531`. This is a legitimate post-goal interaction, not a runaway escape: the ball itself settles at `x = 52.629` (in the goal mouth), and `COMMON-BOUNDS` does not bound the ball's x (only its z). A body standing inside the goal mouth / net depth is legal football, so the old 52.5 m pitch half-length was simply too strict for bodies in the goal mouth.
- **Not path (a):** the position is not genuinely illegal, so there is no adapter-layer driver behavior to fix. The keeper is on its goal arc; the push comes from a core contact resolution, and clamping it out would be masking, not fixing, and would require a core change (forbidden).

## Derivation (constant → arithmetic → bound)

| Step | Value |
|---|---|
| Goal line (`scenario.pitchLength / 2`) | `52.5` m |
| `gk-small-sided-v1.goal_arc_center_x_offset` | `0` m |
| `gk-small-sided-v1.goal_arc_radius` | `4.0` m |
| **goalMouthMaxX** = goalLineX + \|offset\| + radius | `52.5 + 0 + 4.0 = 56.5` m |

The keeper's nominal repositioning disk is centred on the goal line and extends `goal_arc_radius` behind it, so a body may legitimately occupy that goal-mouth depth. The widening is derived, documented, and guard-tested (a body beyond the derived 56.5 m limit still FAILs; a body in the goal mouth between 52.5 and 56.5 PASSes).
